Article Quizzer

Comprehension quizzes for what you read.

How it works
  1. Open an article or a PDF.
  2. Click the Article Quizzer icon in the toolbar, press Alt+Shift+Q, or right-click the page and choose Create a quiz from this page. Pin the icon from Chrome's extensions menu to keep it in reach.
  3. Answer the questions. Each answer includes an explanation and the passage it is based on; Show in article highlights that passage on the page.
  4. Return when a quiz is due for review. Spaced retakes are what make reading stay with you.

Gemini API key

Quizzes are written by Google's Gemini. Keys are free: create one in Google AI Studio, then paste it below.

Model

Privacy

Article Quizzer has no server and collects nothing. It reads a page only after you invoke it there, and if the tab moves to another page, it waits until you invoke it again. When you begin a quiz, the article's text goes directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API. So do your written answers, with the passage behind each question, when they are graded, and the text around an answer when you select Explain more. Your key, settings, and quiz history stay in this browser.

On Gemini's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. Avoid quizzing yourself on confidential documents with a free key.
